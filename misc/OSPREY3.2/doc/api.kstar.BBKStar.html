BBKStar¶

Java class: edu.duke.cs.osprey.kstar.BBKStar

Implementation of the BBK* algorithm to predict protein sequence mutations that improve binding affinity by computing provably accurate Boltzmann-weighted ensembles [Ojewole2008].

[Ojewole2008]Adegoke A. Ojewole, Jonathan D. Jou, Vance G. Fowler, Jr., and Bruce R. Donald, 2017. BBK* (Branch and Bound over K*): A Provable and Efficient Ensemble-Based Algorithm to Optimize Stability and Binding Affinity over Large Sequence Spaces In Research in Computational Molecular Biology (accepted, in press).
